## Conclusion

Support expansion is not the solution. The male triangle `11505` remains inverted for every tested support size, while larger supports increase strain and can create new female inversions. This demonstrates a local orientation/constraint incompatibility rather than a simple boundary-support problem.

No further correction was attempted. Experiment 4 remains failed and Experiment 5 must not start.
